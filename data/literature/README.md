# Literature evidence

`verified_sources.json` bounds the claims supported by the four retained sources. Publisher indexing and Crossref metadata establish the stated limited scope, not complete full-text verification. Access limitations are explicit. Crossref metadata snapshots are in `metadata/`.

`base_reference_catalog.json` preserves all 50 original entries and their disposition; most await independent appraisal. Original numeric values are not imported as properties or validation data. Empty property/validation registries are intentional.

For later numerical extraction record exact page/table/figure, material, units, temperature dependence, extraction method, uncertainty, applicability, and whether used in calibration or independent validation. Do not promote a source based only on title/abstract when the claimed evidence requires methods or numerical results.
