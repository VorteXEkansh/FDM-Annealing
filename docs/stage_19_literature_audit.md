The important additional precedent is Hussam et al., “Effect of annealing on FDM-manufactured ABS parts,” The International Journal of Advanced Manufacturing Technology 138, 2233–2243 (2025), DOI 10.1007/s00170-025-15455-5. Publisher Section 2.2 and Figure 4 describe two aluminium mould clearances (1.0 and 0.6 mm). This invalidates broad novelty based on quantified annealing clearance alone. It concerns ABS, not PLA; no property or recommended clearance is transferred. The source's mechanistic explanations are not adopted as verified polymer physics.
