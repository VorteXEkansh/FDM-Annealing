# Stage 11 independent literature validation protocol

Frozen before any validation solve: 28 September 2026. This is a design and data-admission record, not a validation result. `validation/validation_dataset.csv` is regenerated from immutable primary-source XML by `scripts/build_validation_dataset.py`. Values are source class C, not ANSYS outputs. Blank predictions, errors and response standard deviations are intentional missing evidence, never zero.

## Candidate selection and source verification

The existing 34-paper literature matrix was screened for a directly modeled dimensional observable and a recoverable experimental protocol. DOI identities for the two extracted sources were rechecked against Crossref on this date; the exact responses and primary XML are hashed in `validation/source_manifest.json`. The earlier literature matrix remains a dated Stage 2 snapshot; this protocol is the current validation appraisal.

**Primary: Lluch-Cerezo et al. (2022), DOI 10.3390/polym14132607**, Table 5, with §§2.1–2.4 and Tables 1–3. Ultimaker Pearl White PLA, unidirectional XY+0 roads, 100% infill; rectangular 80 mm × 10 mm × 4 mm bar; 0.2 mm layers and 0.5 mm line width; 0.4 mm nozzle; print temperature 215 °C, bed 60 °C, speed 60 mm s⁻¹. The convection-furnace protocol specifies a 10 °C min⁻¹ ramp, 120 min treatment and cooling in the furnace to room temperature before unpacking. The article describes this cooling explicitly for the mould; equivalence of the mould-free cooling/support path must be confirmed or treated as a protocol uncertainty. Initial ambient temperature, part-temperature attainment, cooling curve, mould-free supporting surface/friction and measurement delay are not resolved.

Table 5 reports signed percentage changes relative to each initial measured dimension, averaged over five specimens per condition. Width and height are averages at three sections. Caliper resolution is 0.01 mm and stated accuracy ±0.03 mm; these are instrument specifications, not a response standard deviation or confidence interval. No raw individual dimensions or within-condition dimensional scatter is supplied in Table 5. Nominal dimensions must not be substituted for each actual initial dimension to manufacture absolute displacements.

Reserve all 18 mould-free directional means at 63, 75, 86, 98, 109 and 132 °C as one external validation set for a **source-specific reproduction**. Three directions from one condition are correlated observables, not three independent experiments. These six temperatures are a source-reproduction domain, not a change to the primary production design. All six groups remain reserved; do not cherry-pick the most favorable temperatures after solving. The 132 °C case requires a source-compatible law and thermal-domain review just as the others do.

All 42 Table 5 PLA values are preserved so the selection is auditable. The 21 powder-supported entries are context only: alumina confinement cannot validate a gap-controlled plate fixture or be represented by an arbitrary clamp. The three mould-free entries at 155 °C are also excluded: 155 °C falls inside the source's stated 145–160 °C melting interval. The earlier Table 7 conflict (240 °C instead of the 155 °C in Tables 2, 3 and 5) is neither silently repaired nor used. Table 5's unambiguous lower-temperature observations can be retained independently of that defect; no effectiveness numbers from Table 7 are imported.

**Secondary: Stojković et al. (2023), DOI 10.3390/ma16134574**, Table 8 and §2. PrimaSelect PLA PRO, 75% cubic infill, ASTM D638-14 dog-bone geometry in Figure 1 (165 mm overall length, 13 mm narrow width, 3.2 mm thickness); nozzle 210 °C, bed 60 °C, speed 50 mm s⁻¹, walls/top/bottom 0.6 mm. Specimens were put in a preconditioned oven over a thin sand layer on steel. Dimensions were measured one hour after printing and one hour after annealing. Road orientation, full part-temperature history and cooling environment remain unresolved. The geometry cannot be replaced by the project's solid rectangular coupon.

The extracted secondary records are the reported PLA maxima: width 0.7 mm at 0.1 mm layer/60 min/100 °C; thickness 0.25 mm at 0.1 mm/30 min/100 °C; length 5.8 mm at 0.1 mm/90 min/100 °C. They are selected extremes, not condition means. The pooled average and standard deviation in Table 8 combine process conditions and cannot be assigned to these individual cases. The source's percent equations use a final-dimension denominator, while Table 8 is in millimetres and does not establish the signs of its positive maxima. Do not reinterpret them as signed shrinkage. These three records are quarantined for descriptive context until individual signed measurements, selection semantics and sufficient protocol information are recovered. This is a genuine second published dataset, but not a second admitted quantitative validation set.

Other matrix candidates: Bute2024 has directional recovery under two 30–140 °C TMA cycles with a probe, not free furnace annealing; force/cooling and curve extraction need additional work. Wijnen2018's reviewed abstract is insufficient for a source-specific solve. Trofimov2022 primarily validates deposition thermal/distortion response, not annealing. Butt2020 remains a fallback; full evidence recovery was unsuccessful in this stage (publisher download returned HTTP 403/429). Chapuis2025 is the calibration lineage and cannot become external validation merely because a new figure is selected.

## Calibration versus validation split

`validation/calibration_register.csv` freezes the existing calibration lineage. Chapuis2025 DMA, master-curve/Maxwell and shift parameters are inherited constitutive calibration. Its bilayer curvature-to-pre-strain fitting and Table 5 fitted pre-strains are calibration-related and excluded from independent validation. The published fit was performed by the source authors; this project has not fitted these parameters anew. The external Table 5 mould-free observations have not been used to estimate any project parameter. Neither have the quarantined Table 8 values.

All observations have already been viewed for appraisal, so the reservation is prospective relative to fitting and solving, not a blinded test. Different papers and commercial formulations do not by themselves establish transferable material validity. The 2022 observations are independent of the existing Prusament calibration lineage, but validating them requires a separate Ultimaker material model supported by independent characterization. Do not transfer Prusament coefficients or fit initial eigenstrain, convection, contact, cooling or crystallization to these reserved outcomes. If any reserved observation informs model selection or tuning, reclassify the entire affected condition and correlated directions as calibration; revise this protocol before solving and obtain a new independent validation set. A random split of rows is prohibited.

## Preregistered reproduction and response mapping

Before any solve, freeze a case manifest containing source material identity, complete bar geometry, layer/road axes, constitutive inputs and their independent provenance, initial printed state, heating/holding/cooling history, observation temperature/time, gravity/support, solver version and production-specific convergence. Preserve each missing item explicitly. Documentary geometry and oven schedule are adequate to define the intended primary reproduction, but missing thermal/material/support closures prevent a unique currently executable validation solve. No case is admitted by zero-filling those fields.

Compare the cooled, supported or released state that matches the source; do not use the hot displacement or substitute the idealized production FREE boundary for an unknown supporting surface. Report signed relative length, width and height changes using the same initial dimensions and section measurement convention. Map the finite-element extraction to those landmarks and sections. Dimensional height change is not warpage. These sources supply no usable residual-stress, contact-pressure, warpage-field or transient-temperature observations; those quantities remain unvalidated. No ANSYS validation or new production sweep runs in Stage 11.

## Metrics fixed before solving

For matched values pᵢ and yᵢ: signed error eᵢ = pᵢ − yᵢ; absolute error aᵢ = |eᵢ|; relative error rᵢ = 100aᵢ/|yᵢ| only when yᵢ ≠ 0. At zero, leave relative error undefined and report absolute error. Near zero, absolute error is primary and the large relative value is flagged, not clipped. For a prespecified group, MAE = Σaᵢ/n and RMSE = √(Σeᵢ²/n). `analysis/validation_metrics.py` implements these equations and is tested with explicitly synthetic arithmetic inputs. It produces no validation result until actual matched predictions exist.

Compute primary summaries separately for L, W and H across the six temperature groups. Signed percentages have absolute errors in **percentage points**, whereas relative errors are percentages. Do not pool millimetres and percentages, directions with different metrology, different formulations or the selected maxima into a global score. Report every individual error and the worst absolute error, with the included condition IDs. Six group means per direction do not supply specimen-level residuals. Do not calculate R², infer sampling confidence, or treat the 18 means as independent replicates. Secondary maxima receive no MAE/RMSE score while quarantined.

## Acceptance interpretation and stopping rules

No unsupported universal error percentage is labeled a validation tolerance. Before any future run, establish response-specific numerical error and measurement bounds in the same units from genuine convergence and metrology evidence, then freeze them in the case manifest. Use the conservative deterministic sum of these bounds to classify each absolute discrepancy as compatible with the declared bounds or exceeding them. Compatibility is not a statistical confidence statement or proof of physical validity. Bounds cannot be enlarged after inspecting prediction errors; model-input uncertainty is reported separately and cannot absorb a mismatch by tuning.

When either bound is unavailable, the mandatory classification is **indeterminate: missing uncertainty**, even for an apparently small error. Current Table 5 lacks the individual initial dimensions and response scatter needed for a defensible complete comparison uncertainty, so no pass/fail validation claim is presently available. A later calculation may report descriptive errors with that limitation but must not call the model validated. Unknown source boundary histories require independently supported bounded scenarios; without them, the reproduction remains blocked. Rejected cases and reasons stay in the record. Any successful source-specific comparison has only that observable/material/protocol domain and does not validate Prusament, the opposed-plate fixture, contact pressure, residual stress or optimal annealing conditions.
