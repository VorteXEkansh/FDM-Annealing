# Journal decision — 31 October 2026

**Conditional target: Progress in Additive Manufacturing (PIAM), Research Paper. No submission recommended for the original predictive annealing objective at present.** Polymer process simulation and component deformation fit the [journal scope](https://link.springer.com/journal/40964/aims-and-scope). The journal has published a directly relevant PLA distortion study [Wijnen et al.](https://doi.org/10.1007/s40964-018-0052-4). A shorter technical contribution limited to a distinct, transferable verification method is a possible alternative only if its advance over existing methods is established on its own merits. A change of journal would not validate the physical model.

*Polymer Testing* would make more sense with substantive new characterization or a validated testing method; a suite of numerical checks is not that. *Polymer Engineering & Science* is plausible for a grade-specific physically validated constitutive result. *Rapid Prototyping Journal*, *Journal of Manufacturing Processes* and *International Journal of Advanced Manufacturing Technology* are plausible process venues once matched FREE/GAP data establish a manufacturing consequence. These are editorial assessments, not editorial decisions or acceptance predictions.

The [PIAM instructions](https://link.springer.com/journal/40964/submission-guidelines) were reviewed for this assessment. For an eventual Research Paper, target 2,000–4,000 words, a 150–250-word abstract, and four to six keywords. Use bracketed numeric citations, consecutively numbered references with full DOI links, editable equations and tables, and decimal headings (at most three levels). Provide data availability and the required statements on interests, contributions and funding. Keep a neutral named master; prepare a separate anonymized review copy and follow the submission system's current fields for identities and declarations. Disclose substantive use of language models beyond copy editing and obtain human author sign-off. Consult current instructions again at submission.

The earlier release has seven keywords and repeats unavailable production outputs as separate Results subsections. Collapse these into the validity boundary. Put coefficient inventories, detailed error tables and verification attempts in SI. Do not create figure captions or results subsections for nonexistent campaigns. An anonymized journal submission variant, author declarations and any submission action are deferred; the repository manuscript is the publisher-neutral version. PIAM's scope page announces an open-access transition and an acceptance-contingent charge for submissions received from 11 August 2026 (subject to applicable waiver); no financial commitment is made.
