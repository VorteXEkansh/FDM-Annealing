# Master scientific audit: intake and admissible next steps

Reviewed 30–31 October 2026. Baseline is `d23a9ecd097072bf8165a36fa731e8e15910122f`. The intake audit covers the scientific repository by component, including both complete release PDFs (main pp. 1–18, SI pp. 1–21), the editable manuscript source, source and property matrices, case/attempt records, verification and convergence tables, validation registers, production/optimization registries, environment and reproducibility manifests. All pages of the two baseline PDFs were text-extracted and read; the separate original visual review is recorded in `docs/stage_20_visual_review.json`. That original inspection does not certify any later revisions. This is an internal adverse review, not external peer review, and does not entail repeating previously verified ANSYS calculations.

## Decision sequence

**First decide what is identifiable.** Select grade, colour where relevant, road architecture, specimen section and thermal interval. For an oven-response task select grade-specific thermal functions and separate oven, part and fixture histories. A measured part history admits a narrower structural-only task. Identify irreversible strain or initial printed state from independent time-resolved directional data. If these remain missing, stop physical prediction there; separately continue tests whose inputs are mathematically prescribed.

**Determine whether restraint is possible.** Track local free surface envelope relative to both faces, plate spacing versus temperature, initial scan, spacers and rigid-motion/support convention. Thickness can increase during annealing even as length contracts; the sign is not universal. Zero nominal clearance is not preload. A normal stop may prevent bending without transmitting appreciable in-plane force. Unknown friction, contact conductance, plate bending or gradient cannot be replaced with values chosen to produce a benefit. Symmetric compatible free strain in a flat homogeneous coupon is not a warpage-generating mechanism.

**Verify before interpreting.** Verify a transient clock independently; for a genuinely physical coupled 3-D case verify heat balance, contact status/reaction/penetration, free rigid modes, selected mesh/time increments over heating, hold, cooling and release. Test actual extraction semantics. Compare quantities in fixed physical zones away from edge singularities and acknowledge stress observability limits. No input defect requiring a rerun of the pre-existing accepted benchmarks was found in this audit.

**Validate independently, then design.** Admit matched grade, architecture, support, history, release, metrology and source uncertainty. Freeze calibration, hold out conditions/physical builds, and define observable-specific acceptance before predictions. Only then compare matched FREE/GAP cases, distinguish process from material uncertainty, evaluate out-of-sample surrogates, optimize justified objectives and confirm new points. Efficient numerical solvers do not bypass material identification or validation.

## Review verdict

- **Polymer science:** No evidence supports extending the entire Prusament law to 95/110 °C, nor turning a bilayer programmed pre-strain into homogeneous bulk shrinkage. Lack of identifiability is not evidence of zero recovery.
- **Computational mechanics:** Restrained/released contact needs full kinematics and reaction balance; geometry agreement alone does not validate stress, area or pressure. Keep numerical benchmark names clear and compact.
- **Additive manufacturing:** The first-layer direction and toolpath can matter even with a nominally symmetric shape; the free curl reversal test in Lorenzoni is a useful discriminator. Do not manufacture an unmeasured through-thickness mismatch to get a curve.
- **Numerics and statistics:** Keep accepted runs distinct from failed attempts and refinement measurements distinct from physical replicates. A holdout should exclude an entire correlated condition, not alternate rows of an exported curve.
- **Editorial:** A benchmark/feasibility contribution can be stated positively; no experimentally validated processing region or publication-readiness certification follows. Keep the unresolved manufacturing question visible, reduce repeated absences and include an accurate author-verified contribution/AI-use statement before any submission. See `master_journal_fit.md`.
