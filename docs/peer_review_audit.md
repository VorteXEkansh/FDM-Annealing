## Editorial decision

Major scientific work remains necessary before a production annealing article can be submitted. The project has useful numerical benchmarks but no executable, physically validated coupled PLA annealing model, admitted production cases, sensitivity/UQ, optimization or independent confirmation. These omissions cannot be repaired by prose or an arbitrary solver run. The current PDF is an audited verification-focused draft; the title and abstract now explicitly separate the component tests. The numerical audit does not resolve the missing physical evidence.

## Equation and unit audit

Equations 1–2 reproduce geometry and bounded linear interpolation. Equation 3 is the implemented effective instantaneous/delayed/thermal split, not an unimplemented irreversible law. Equations 4–5 use tensor shear, dimensionless strain and MPa stiffness; isotropic ν bounds are enforced. Equations 6–8 preserve positive branch moduli and the same fractional bulk/shear spectra. Equation 9 matches the source UMAT's base-10 clock; reciprocal temperatures use kelvin and temperature differences use kelvin-equivalent degrees Celsius. Equation 10 is reduced-time quadrature; Equation 11 uses stable exponential increments and a midpoint-temperature approximation, not an exact arbitrary-temperature update.

Equation 12 is the constant-property conduction benchmark only. Equation 13 is force balance with the corrected configuration-specific interpretation. Equation 14 uses positive separation and compression; finite-penalty penetration is a numerical approximation. Equation 15 now maps to the explicit zero-denominator implementation. Equation 16 uses plane-wall roots and an ambient-history convolution; reported errors refer only to the nine samples. Equation 17 has correct compressive axial stress, left-reaction sign and Poisson response for its transverse freedom. Equation 18 superposes loading/unloading finite-ramp memory at 65 °C. Equations 19–21 agree with the implemented metrics; percentage-change errors use percentage points, and the bound screen is not statistical acceptance or physical validation.

All 21 equations remain tied to executed reference/calculation code. No ornamental product operator or ± sign is inserted merely to satisfy a symbol checklist. Existing √, ∑, ∂, ∇, ×, degrees, exponents and indices are inspected where used. Numerical pressure and modulus units are MPa; contact reaction per unit out-of-plane depth is N mm⁻¹; gradient differences may be expressed in K m⁻¹.
